About Us

Get to Know Konni Consult and Our IT Solutions.

Based in Mbezi Beach Massana, Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam, Konni Consult has spent more than 5 years helping businesses build the technology backbone they need to operate with confidence. From IT consulting and cloud computing to computer networking and GIS solutions, our team delivers practical, dependable expertise across every layer of your IT environment. We work closely with clients to understand their goals, then design and implement solutions that keep systems running smoothly and securely, whether that means migrating to the cloud, optimizing a network, or managing complex data.

Beyond consulting, we provide hands-on support that keeps your operations moving — from data entry and IT support to hardware and software troubleshooting. Our commitment is to be a responsive, trusted partner, not just a service provider. With more than 5 years of experience serving clients both within Dar es Salaam and beyond, Konni Consult brings the technical know-how and attentive support to solve whatever IT challenge comes your way, wherever you're located.
